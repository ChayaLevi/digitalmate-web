## Decisions made during the build

Record decisions that future chats need and that are not in `docs/PLAN.md`.

- T9.1: **a preset is a reset made at the scene's moment**, not a reset plus a clock jump: the seed is built with `now` = the target time and `clockOffsetMs` = target − real now, so "today's pickup" is on the scene's day. Targets (`server/homemind/presets.ts`): Scene 1 and 3 the next weekday 14:30 (pickup 16:30); Scene 2 the next Monday 10:00 + claims on (Mom, level 4); Scene 4 the next Sunday 12:00 (pizza shop open, so Confirm works at once); Scene 5 the next Monday 19:30 + claims on + the first two group messages posted. "Next" is at or after the real now, at most 8 days ahead. The seed itself is unchanged.
- T9.1: **tests run at a fixed time.** e2e resets with `{weekday: 3, time: '14:30'}` (the next Wednesday 14:30) before every test. `tests/homemindBridge.test.ts` fakes `Date` only (still moving) at Wednesday 2026-11-11 14:30 in the machine's zone (`tests/homemindFixedTime.ts`). Item 4 touched only tests and the reset route that item 1 needed, not the seed.
- T9.1: **"Jump to next reminder"** (`POST /demo/next-reminder`, `nextDueAt`) goes to the earliest of: a reminder's next attempt, its next escalation step (from `firstAt`), or an evening summary that has something waiting. 409 `nothing_due` otherwise.
- T9.1: **rule edits**: `POST /rules` takes `update` (`maxPrice` 1–5000, `time` HH:MM), `delete` and `restore` (`activityId`). Update and delete withdraw the rule's waiting cards (visibly: "withdrawn: the rule was changed" / "turned off"); update also drops the runs for occurrences still ahead, so the next rule step prepares a card from the new rule. Delete keeps the rule in `Activity.ruleUndo`; restore puts it back once (409 after). Parents only. The details screen is `GET /screen/rules?id=`.
- T9.1: the 接龙 sample has **no printed dates** (the plan's other option was regenerating it per demo week).
- T9.1: one **prompt line** in `intents.ts`: being away for a planned stretch is `create_event` with `unavailableMemberIds`, not `report_situation` (the live model broke Scene 4 without it).
- T8.3: **private to the mailbox owners.** New `Item.mailboxIds` (set only by `inbox.ts`; the core accepts it only on a proposed item, with the actor among the owners and all ids real members). `core.canSee` returns false for anyone not listed, so **not even the other parent** sees it (every screen, search, activity, Why and SSE event goes through `canSee`; `eventFor` sends them nothing, not even a removal). **Approve clears `mailboxIds`**, so it becomes an ordinary family item; Undo of the approval brings it back (the old version). Dismiss keeps the item cancelled and private. A client cannot send `mailboxIds`.
- T8.3: **one item per mail.** The mail's id and version are the item's `sourceRefs`. If a live (not cancelled) item from that mail exists, a second add, by the same person or the other parent, creates nothing (`duplicate: true`) and returns it. A mail in both parents' mailboxes gives `mailboxIds: [mom, dad]`, so either can see and approve the one proposal. A dismissed one can be added again.
- T8.3: **what a mail asks is in the fixture, not read by a model** (`MailAsk` in `fakes.ts`): the class photo mail is a task for next Wednesday (all day), the library mail a task for tomorrow, the pool mail an event next Saturday; the quote is the item's note. Dates come from the demo clock. The source is `{kind: 'email', label: '<sender> (sample email)', simulated: true}`. The sale mail from a sender not on the approved list is never shown and `POST /inbox/mail-4/add` is 404.
- T8.3: **the route** `POST /inbox/:mailId/add {}`: signed in with an account, the mail must be in that member's own mailbox (else 404, so nobody learns what is in another one); `409 nothing_to_add` for a mail with no request. Services shows one "Add to my board: <subject>" button per mail not on the board and "On your board" on the others. No Why record yet for these items (the source label and the quoted note say where it came from).

- T8.2: **the tier of a group message** (`judgeText`, fixed rules, in order, never the model): urgent words (紧急, 马上, 立刻, 立即, 尽快, 停课, 提前放学, urgent, immediately, asap…) → `urgent`; the 接龙 list itself (`#接龙` or a line starting with 接龙; a message that only *asks* people to sign up is not one) → `timed`; the sender's own "不着急 / 不急 / no rush / not urgent" → `summary` (wins over a date); today, tonight, tomorrow, this week (本周/这周) → `timed`; next week or month → `summary`; a weekday (周四, Friday) → `timed`; nothing → `summary` ("no date"). The words matched are kept as `quote` and shown in the reason.
- T8.2: **who is alerted**: `urgent` and `timed` group messages alert the **parents with an account** only (it is the parents' group), as a new alert kind `message` (`itemId` = the message id, `simulated: true`). A sign-up that T7.4 just **claimed** sends no second alert (`handledBy: 'group-claim'`). `summary` sends nothing; parents see it on My day ("Waiting for tonight's summary", with the reason and "No alert was sent for these") and on the Services row. A family reply is not judged. Edits (T7.5) are not judged again.
- T8.2: **the evening summary** goes out when a tick **crosses** 20:00 local (`summaryCheckedAt`, set at seed and reset). A server started after 20:00 does not send that evening's; a clock jump over several evenings sends only the latest. One `summary` notice per member with an account (tier `timed`, so quiet hours hold it; never batched). The messages that waited are marked `deliveredIn`. On the evening before `weekStartsOn` (Sunday), `weekAhead: true`.
- T8.2: **what is in a summary** (`summaryFor`, from the member's own `visibleItems`): daily = today and tomorrow; weekly = the 7 days after today (a preview on any day, "sent" on Sunday evening). "What you need to do": theirs (`owner`), given and not confirmed (`unconfirmed`), "Who's taking this?" they could take (`asked`, same rule as `needsOwner`), a reminder to them that got no answer (`unanswered`, from T4.1's `summaryAt`), plus undated tasks of theirs. Then who is away (`unavailabilityFor`, so a Parents-only away event leaves no trace for a teen), then every other event in the period ("For everyone"), then (parents, daily only) the group messages that waited.
- T8.2: **itemless alerts**: `message` and `summary` are not about a board item. `isItemlessAlert` (alerts.ts): the hub delivers them to any member with an account the maker chose; held ones still apply after quiet hours; they are never batched with item updates; the client keeps them as notices (Open: Services or Summary; Dismiss).
- T8.2: **correcting a saved preference** (Scene 5): the Memory tab has **Correct** next to Remove (same rights: a soft one anyone with an account, a hard one a parent). It opens `memory?id=<prefId>`: two fields (the words, the reason) and "Save the correction", which sends `POST /preferences {id, text, reason, strength, aboutMemberId, scope}` (T5.4's correction by id). New words drop the old Chinese text. The sign-up choices and the next claim read preferences live, so the next suggestion changes at once.

- Fix after T8.1 (human's review): **not every event needs an owner.** `needsOwner(item)` in `homemindSurfaces.ts`: open items with ownership `open`, or `none` when the item is a task or preparation **or** an event marked Important (the pickup). The same line `askFor` in `reminders.ts` already drew. An event with `none` that is not Important (a wedding, a class party) has **participants only**: its row shows who it is for (`forMemberIds`), no state label, muted, and no "I'll take it" / "Assign to" on list rows. Its own screen (item and edit) still offers them, so a parent can give it an owner on purpose. The Add form's owner choice says "Nobody (an event can have only participants)". The core is unchanged.
- Fix after T8.1: **My day is by day.** Sections: Today (everything on today, including a multi-day event covering today, and what waits for an approval, an owner or this member's "I'm on it"), Coming up (the next 7 days), and last "Still waiting: no date or not this week" (waiting items undated, from an earlier day, or later). The old top "Needs your answer" (every unowned item from every day) is gone. Shown on a member's day: what they own, what is for them, what is for nobody in particular (no `forMemberIds`, no owner), and for a parent a child's item while nobody owns it.
- T8.1: **what falls through** (`planSituation`): the sick member's **events** that start between now and the end of that local day (one already started is left alone), and items **assigned to** that member then. Read from the **reporter's** view (`canSeeItem`), so a teen's report never names a Parents-only item. Rules, in order: a weekly event → `skip` (one occurrence); a title with pickup/drop-off words (`PICKUP_PATTERN`, EN and ZH) → `cancel` ("not needed, she stays home"); any other event → `postpone` to the same local time 7 days later; an owned item → `open` for everyone else who could own it. Tasks and preparations *for* the member are not touched.
- T8.1: **who stays home**: only for a sick **child**. Candidates are the parents with an account (not the sick one) who have no "away" event that day in the reporter's view (`unavailabilityFor`). The task is created at once as "Who's taking this?" (`open`, so the existing `open` alert reaches the other parent) when chat may write (level 2+, not paused), else `proposed`. Homemind never picks between parents and compares availability only, never workload (CLAUDE.md). Nobody free: both parents are asked anyway, and a daytime babysitter draft (first free on the list, from now or 09:00 to 17:00) is prepared. The task is all-day, so it has no reminders (reminders.ts skips all-day items).
- T8.1: **suggestions wait for a tap at every autonomy level**, even 4: they cancel or move things people planned. Apply uses the item's **current** version (a confirmation in between does not spoil it) but refuses `409 changed` if the item moved since the suggestion; `Apply all` applies each as its own core change (own Activity, own Undo) and reports the ones that no longer fit. "Not now" changes nothing on the board. Undo: the core Undo of that change, and the suggestion waits again. Anyone with an account may decide (as for any family item); the core refuses an item the member can't see (404).
- T8.1: **the same member and day reported twice** returns the existing situation (no second task or card). One `Situation` per report in `state.situations` (at most 30), shown from the report until 12 hours after its day ends.
- T8.1: **the teacher message**: new simulated adapter `teacher-message` (area `childcare`, never at level 4 on its own), in Chinese, as "诺娅妈妈/爸爸" (the reporter if a parent, else the first free parent): "王老师您好！诺娅今天身体不舒服，在家休息，不来幼儿园了。谢谢！". No symptoms, nothing about the parents. Only for a child, only Monday to Friday, expires at the end of that day. The simulated teacher answers with a canned line; sent messages show on **Services** ("Messages to the class teacher").
- T8.1: **privacy**: a situation's view per member keeps only suggestions and away entries about items that member can see; the reporter's own words (`summary`) go to parents and the reporter only. Its `Why` names the items and the away events in `basis`, so a teen does not get the Why at all when one of them is Parents-only (`viewFor` rule).
- T8.1: chat `report_situation` other than `sick` (`away`, `late`, `other`) still answers "not built"; "away" is what an event with "Who is away" already does (T1.3/T5.2). "I'm sick" (no member named) is about the member who wrote it.

- T7.5: **what an item depends on.** Items, claims and cards carry `sourceRefs: [{id, version}]` (the plan's `sourceRef`, as a list): a claim's party and preparations depend on **both** the sign-up and the teacher's message the time came from (`refsOf`), so either edit counts. Items from older data without refs fall back to the claim's own `eventId`/`preparationIds`. Only group sign-ups make items from messages today; inbox messages already have an id and a version, but nothing is created from them yet (T8.3).
- T7.5: **comparing.** `compareReading` re-reads the sign-up with `readSignUp` (fixed rules, as T7.4) and compares in code: `eventStart`, `deadline`, `options` (removed/added labels), and `choice` (the family's choice left the list or someone else took it; `switchTo` is what T7.4's own rules would claim instead: food to buy, within the cap, no hard preference against it). A version that can't be read is `unclear` (Apply refused, `409 unclear`). No difference (wording only): versions are updated quietly, nothing is flagged. A stopped claim (nothing on the board) is just read again. **A claim is never made twice for the same message**: `claimFor` now matches the message id regardless of version.
- T7.5: **marking.** One core change `source_sync` (new `Change` kind, actor `homemind`, the only change that accepts it) sets `Item.stale = {changeId, field: 'start'|'choice'|'unclear', oldValue, newValue, reason, reasonZh}` on every live dependent item, in one Activity (`kind: 'source'`, `note: 'marked'`). Pending cards that quote the old version get `held`; `authorize` refuses a held card with the new code `held` (409) before anything else, for a tap and for level 4. A held card on My day says why and has no Confirm button. `tickReminders` skips firing reminders of a stale item (they stay scheduled and fire once cleared, if still due). One `SourceChange` per claim (`state.sourceChanges`, at most 50; a newer edit replaces a pending one), with a `Why` (`source: 'rule'`) that says it was compared in code.
- T7.5: **the notice.** New alert kind `stale` (`tier: 'timed'`, a notice, Open/Dismiss) to each member with an account who **owns or is asked about** an affected item they can see, with `staleCount` (their items) and `staleWhat`/`staleWhatZh` (the change in plain words). Neutral; sensitive items show "You have a reminder". The client parser requires `staleCount`, `staleWhat` and `simulated: true`.
- T7.5: **deciding** (`POST /source-changes/:id/apply|keep|discard`, **parents only**: Apply and Discard may post in the group). Apply: the event and every flagged item move by the event's shift (end too), flags clear, refs follow the new version, the claim takes the new times and choices; if the family's choice left the list, a correction card (`prepareClaimCorrection`, the T7.4 "Change" code, now also able to take the family off the list with `to: ''`) is prepared and run at level 4 through the guard: "更正：诺娅妈妈认领改为橙子（原来是苹果，苹果已不需要）", or "更正：诺娅妈妈取消认领苹果（…）" if nothing else fits. A waiting claim card is withdrawn and the sign-up read again (new card). Keep: flags clear, times stay, held cards are released.
